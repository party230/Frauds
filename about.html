How a Fraud Is Graded

Every ranked figure is judged on ten distinct qualities, all split evenly in the aggregate. No quality outweighs another, and no Fraud is exempt from any of them.

The Cycle

What happens between refreshes

Step 01

Evidence

The panel collects the cycle's material: appearances, ventures, transformation updates and conduct across the verse.

Step 02

Grading

Each of the ten qualities is graded independently out of 100. The aggregate is the flat mean — nothing is weighted, nothing is rounded in anyone's favour.

Step 03

Community input

Rising star and overrated votes are counted alongside the panel grades. Sustained sentiment in one direction triggers a review of the affected qualities.

FAQ

How often does the board move?

The hierarchy is refreshed every 48 hours. Movement is driven by panel grades and by community input on whether a Fraud is a rising star or overrated.

Can I add someone to the rankings?

Yes. Verified members may nominate one figure per cycle through the submission form. The panel requires two cycles of evidence before a nomination enters the board.

Why did a Fraud drop without changing?

The board is relative. A Fraud can hold every grade and still fall if the figures beneath them improved faster in the same cycle.

Can a grade be appealed?

Panel Access members may file one appeal per cycle. Appeals must name the specific quality being disputed and cite evidence from the cycle in question.

Is height really worth as much as business acumen?

Yes. All ten qualities are worth exactly ten percent. That even split is the whole basis of the ranking and it is not adjusted for individual cases.

Are these real people?

No. Every figure on this board is fictional and every grade is invented. This site is a parody of leaderboard culture.
