Privacy Notice

Last updated 24 August 2026. Short, because this site collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no backend, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no cookies. Nothing you type here is transmitted anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Your account, membership plan, watchlist, votes, nominations and appeals are written to localStorage in your own browser under the key ofr.state.v2. That data never leaves your device and is not readable by us or anyone else.

Deleting it

Clear it in one click from your dashboard, or clear site data in your browser. Both remove everything permanently — there is no copy anywhere to restore from.

Third parties

The only external request this site makes is to Google Fonts for two typefaces. If your browser blocks it, the site falls back to system fonts and works exactly the same.
