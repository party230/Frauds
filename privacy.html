Privacy Notice

Last updated 24 August 2026. Short, because this site collects nothing.

What we collect

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and one cookie: the session that keeps you signed in. With the backend running, your account, membership, watchlist, votes, grades, saved articles, routine and nominations are stored in its database. Served as plain static files, none of that leaves your browser at all.

What is stored, and where

With a backend: rows in its SQLite database, keyed to your account, plus a session cookie holding a random token (the database stores only its hash). Without one: localStorage in your own browser under the key ofr.state.v2, which never leaves your device.

Deleting it

Delete your account in one click from your dashboard — it removes the account row and, by cascade, every vote, grade, bookmark, routine day, nomination and appeal attached to it. Clearing site data does the same in the browser-only mode. Neither is recoverable.

Third parties

The only external request this site makes is to Google Fonts for two typefaces. If your browser blocks it, the site falls back to system fonts and works exactly the same.
