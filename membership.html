Membership FAQ

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your dashboard. Membership runs to the end of the cycle you have paid for and then stops.

Does membership buy a ranking?

No. Members nominate figures for review; the panel grades them. Nothing on this board is for sale, including a place on it.

What is a nomination worth?

One figure per member per cycle goes in front of the panel with your case attached. The panel still needs two cycles of evidence before anything is graded.

Is this a real payment?

No. This is a demonstration site — the checkout takes no money and no card details leave the form.
